## Initial Episode Suitability Checklist

This document is designed to evalute 5 episodes from each podcast to see if they are worth including. This is to minimise time listening to load of podcasts that are not suitable for the model and meet the criteria outlines

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| C1 | A specific company is mentioned |
| C2 | The company is listed on the London Stock Exchange |
| C3 | A clear buy recommendation is made |
| C4 | The episode has a verifiable publication date |
| C5 | The recommendation can be verified using audio, a transcript or a timestamp |
| C6 | Historical share-price data is available |
| C7 | Enough historical data exists after publication to evaluate the selected holding periods |
