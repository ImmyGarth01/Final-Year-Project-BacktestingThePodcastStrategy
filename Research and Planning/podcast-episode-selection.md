# Podcast Episode Selection

**Purpose:** To identify suitable financial podcast episodes for backtesting investment recommendations involving London Stock Exchange-listed companies.

## Episode Suitability Checklist

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| C1 | A specific company is mentioned |
| C2 | The company is listed on the London Stock Exchange |
| C3 | A clear buy recommendation is made |
| C4 | The episode has a verifiable publication date |
| C5 | The recommendation can be verified using audio, a transcript or a timestamp |
| C6 | Historical share-price data is available |
| C7 | Enough historical data exists after publication to evaluate the selected holding periods |

## Decision Rules

- **Suitable:** All seven criteria are satisfied.
- **Pending:** One or more criteria still need investigating.
- **Unsuitable:** At least one essential criterion is not satisfied.

**Key:** ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ❓ Uncertain

## Additional Notes

For each suitable recommendation, record:

- The speaker's name.
- The exact recommendation or a brief summary.
- The audio timestamp or transcript reference.
- Whether the speaker disclosed owning shares in the company.
- Any relevant disclaimer.

**Important:** A general disclaimer does not automatically make an episode unsuitable. Only explicit buy recommendations will be included in the initial backtesting dataset.
